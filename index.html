Frequently Asked Questions

Have doubts about how we coordinate, evaluate, and award book typing? We compiled clear answers to queries our new writers commonly present.

What exactly is the Work-From-Home book writing work?
We provide high-quality scanned copies or drafts of hand-written books, publications, and reports. Your job is to accurately type them out onto a digital document (Word/Docs) or write them neatly on pre-provided project pages.
Is there any registration fee or security deposit required?
No, we strictly do not charge any registration fees or hidden payments to start working. We believe in building a safe and trusted writer community.
How and when will I receive my reward/payment?
Payments are processed instantly within 24 to 48 hours after your completed book project is successfully submitted and verified by our quality assurance team. You can choose to be paid directly via UPI, GooglePay, PhonePe, or Bank Transfer.
What happens if I cannot complete the work within the duration?
Our project durations are generous and designed with part-time writers in mind. If you ever require an extension due to emergencies, you can contact our support team to extend your timeline by 2-3 additional days.
Do I need a laptop or computer, or can I write from my mobile phone?
You can easily complete most projects using a smartphone. We support typing in simple mobile text editors or using Google Docs app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have doubts about how we coordinate, evaluate, and award book typing? We compiled immediate clear answers to queries our new writers commonly present.

What exactly is the Work-From-Home book writing work?
We provide high-quality scanned copies or drafts of hand-written books, publications, and reports. Your job is to accurately type them out onto a digital document (Word/Docs) or write them neatly on pre-provided project pages. It's simple, authentic writing work designed to be done comfortably from home.
Is there any registration fee or security deposit required?
No, we strictly do not charge any registration fees or hidden payments to start working. We believe in building a safe and trusted writer community. Beware of any platforms asking for advance payments in our name.
How and when will I receive my reward/payment?
Payments are processed instantly within 24 to 48 hours after your completed book project is successfully submitted and verified by our quality assurance team. You can choose to be paid directly via UPI, GooglePay, PhonePe, or Bank Transfer.
What happens if I cannot complete the work within the duration?
Our project durations (ranging from 7 days up to 26 days) are quite generous and designed with part-time writers in mind. If you ever require an extension due to emergencies, you can contact our support team on WhatsApp or Telegram to extend your timeline by 2-3 additional days.
Do I need a laptop or computer, or can I write from my mobile phone?
You can easily complete most projects using a smartphone. We support typing in simple mobile text editors or using Google Docs app. For hand-written projects, you only need clean writing supplies and a good camera to upload page scans.
Is this work-from-home writing job safe and legally compliant?
Yes. All our publishing projects are verified. We draft transparent guidelines for each book assignment. Our primary communication channel is our certified Telegram channel, which provides official daily updates and project catalogs.
